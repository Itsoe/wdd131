Edutari Site Plan

Site Name

Edutari

I chose this name because it is connected to education. The site is about helping digitally excluded students access learning through preloaded tablets and offline study resources.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to explain how Edutari supports African students who have limited access to reliable internet and digital learning resources. The site will share information about the learning platform, preloaded tablets, school subjects, exam preparation, student progress, and how parents, schools, and communities can support students.

Scenarios

  • How can my child continue learning if we do not always have internet at home?
  • What subjects and exam preparation materials are available on Edutari?
  • How can parents, schools, or communities support students using Edutari?
