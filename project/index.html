Built for students who cannot rely on constant internet access.

Many students have the motivation to learn, but reliable internet, personal devices, and quality digital learning material are still barriers. Edutari is designed as a practical bridge: learning content is loaded before the tablet reaches the student, so lessons remain available at home, at school, and in community study spaces.

A simple learning cycle

Preload the tablet

Schools or partners load lessons, practice activities, books, and exam materials before devices are distributed.

Study offline

Students open lessons without using mobile data, review examples, complete quizzes, and revisit difficult topics.

Track progress

Parents and teachers can review completed work and identify subjects where a learner needs extra support.

Designed for real learning conditions.

Edutari focuses on the environments students already live in. Lessons can be used during power interruptions, in shared homes, in small classrooms, or in community centers where internet may only be available occasionally.

  • Offline lessons for repeated study
  • Low-data updates when connectivity is available
  • Simple navigation for students and guardians
  • Content organized by subject, level, and exam goal
