Support makes offline learning stronger.

Edutari works best when students are surrounded by adults and community partners who can encourage study routines, protect devices, and help connect tablets to updates when internet is available.

Everyone has a part to play

Parents and guardians

Set study time, check weekly progress, charge devices safely, and celebrate completed lessons.

Schools

Choose subject packs, align lessons with classroom topics, and guide students toward exam readiness.

Community partners

Create shared learning spaces, sponsor tablets, and help update content when connectivity is available.

Common questions

Can students use Edutari without internet at home?

Yes. The main learning resources are preloaded onto the tablet so students can study offline.

How do new lessons reach the tablet?

Schools or community centers can update content when the tablet is connected to reliable internet.

How can parents help if they are not subject experts?

Parents can support routines, check completed activities, and encourage students to return to difficult topics.
